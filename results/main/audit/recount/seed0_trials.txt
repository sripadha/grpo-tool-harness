PART 2. Smallest detectable effect: se_halves, mde_lowest_split, mde_highest_split of the 4 arms.
Each way makes 200 splits with numpy default_rng(0). Per split: family pass rate of half A minus
half B; the standard deviation of that over the 48 families (n - 1) / sqrt(48) is the split's value.
Three ways to pool the 200 values into se_halves: mean, root mean square, median.

Note: ways 1, 2, 3 and 6 are one way written four times if they give the same row above: a random
order of the 8 attempts of each scenario, scenarios taken family by family and style by style.
The lowest and highest split can be equal to the table by chance in the GRPO arms even with other
random numbers, because few values are possible there (few scenarios have both passes and fails).
In part 1 a single end can also be equal by chance: the ends sit on a grid of 1/1536.
